# AI Actor Character Specs — Image Generation Reference

**Project:** Personal Health Vault (PHV)
**Purpose:** Five mascot characters representing AI actors in a personal health data platform. Used in the web UI (avatar icons, tab headers, notification badges) and in architecture documentation.

---

## Global Style Guide

**Style:** Exaggerated cartoon, in the tradition of classic character design — think Super Mario, Popeye, Pixar shorts, Cuphead, or Hanna-Barbera. Big heads on small bodies (roughly 2:1 head-to-body ratio). Oversized hands and props. Thick outlines. Bold, saturated colors. Every character should be instantly readable at 64x64 pixels and still charming at full size.

**Medium:** Clean vector illustration, flat or lightly shaded (no photorealism). White or transparent background. Suitable for use as UI avatars and icons.

**Palette:** Each character has a signature color. All share the same warm skin tone and thick black outlines.

**Mood:** Friendly, competent, slightly goofy — these are serious professionals who don't take themselves too seriously. They should feel like a team you'd trust with your health data.

**Consistency:** All five characters should look like they belong in the same cartoon universe. Same line weight, same proportions, same level of exaggeration.

## Character 1: The Narrator

**Role:** Chronicles the patient's medical history. Transforms raw clinical data into a readable, chronological story.

**Signature color:** Blue (#2471A3)

**Appearance:** A stout, cheerful scribe or chronicler. Big round head, warm brown eyes wide with focus, thick expressive eyebrows. Wears a floppy blue beret tilted to one side and a blue tunic or vest with gold buttons. Has an ink smudge on one cheek (endearing, not messy). Small rounded body, stubby legs.

**Props:** Holds an oversized feather quill in one hand — the quill is comically large, almost as tall as the character, with a bright red feather. The other hand holds or rests on an open leather-bound book with visible handwritten lines on the pages. A small ink bottle sits nearby or is tucked into a belt. Drops of ink may be mid-splatter.

**Pose:** Leaning slightly forward, mid-sentence, quill poised over the book as if capturing something important right now. Expression is focused but happy — the face of someone who loves their job.

**Expression:** Wide-eyed concentration with a big open-mouthed grin. One eyebrow slightly raised as if discovering something interesting to write down.

**Prompt snippet:** "Cartoon mascot character, big-headed stout scribe wearing a blue beret, holding an oversized red feather quill and an open book, ink smudge on cheek, cheerful focused expression, thick black outlines, vector illustration style, white background"

## Character 2: The Analyst

**Role:** Hunts for hidden patterns, correlations, and anomalies in the patient's health data. The curious detective-scientist.

**Signature color:** Orange (#D35400)

**Appearance:** A wiry, energetic mad-scientist type. Big head with wild, gravity-defying white or gray hair shooting out in all directions (Einstein meets Doc Brown). Wears thick round glasses that magnify the eyes comically. Has a white lab coat worn open over an orange shirt. Small body practically vibrating with excitement. Bushy eyebrows that are as expressive as the eyes.

**Props:** Holds a comically oversized magnifying glass in one hand — so large it takes up a quarter of the frame, with a glinting lens. The other hand points upward toward a floating lightbulb (the "eureka!" moment). A small chart or graph with a trend line may be visible in a coat pocket or floating nearby. Pens poking out of the lab coat breast pocket.

**Pose:** Dynamic, caught in the act of discovery. Leaning forward with eyes wide behind the magnifying glass, one finger pointing triumphantly at the sky. The whole posture says "AHA! I found something!"

**Expression:** Eyes huge and round behind the glasses (one eye comically magnified by the lens). Mouth wide open in an excited shout. Eyebrows way up. Pure eureka energy.

**Prompt snippet:** "Cartoon mascot character, big-headed mad scientist with wild white Einstein hair, thick round glasses magnifying eyes, white lab coat over orange shirt, holding oversized magnifying glass, pointing up at a glowing lightbulb, excited eureka expression, thick black outlines, vector illustration style, white background"

## Character 3: The Sentinel

**Role:** Vigilant guardian who monitors the medical literature, FDA alerts, and clinical guidelines for anything relevant to the patient. Stands watch 24/7.

**Signature color:** Red (#C0392B)

**Appearance:** A stocky, square-jawed superhero-type. Big determined head with a strong chin and dark swept-back hair. Wears a red outfit with a flowing red cape that billows dramatically behind (even indoors — it's a cartoon). Has a gold belt with a shield-shaped buckle bearing a medical cross. Boots. The whole vibe is "friendly neighborhood health guardian" — heroic but approachable, not intimidating.

**Props:** One hand raised to the brow in a scanning/lookout gesture, peering into the distance. Radar or signal waves emanate from the eyes or from the scanning hand (concentric arcs, getting fainter with distance). May have a small telescope or binoculars clipped to the belt. A subtle medical caduceus or cross emblem on the chest.

**Pose:** Classic superhero lookout stance — feet planted in a wide power stance, one hand shading the eyes while scanning the horizon, the other hand on the hip or holding the cape. Cape flowing to one side. The posture radiates vigilance and readiness.

**Expression:** Determined, alert, but with a reassuring confidence. Slight squint as if focusing on something far away. A small confident smirk — "I've got this. Nothing gets past me."

**Prompt snippet:** "Cartoon mascot character, big-headed stocky superhero with square jaw and dark swept hair, red costume with flowing cape, gold belt with medical cross shield buckle, hand raised scanning the horizon with radar waves emanating, confident determined expression, thick black outlines, vector illustration style, white background"

## Character 4: The Assistant

**Role:** The patient's conversational partner. Answers questions, explains findings, helps prepare for doctor appointments. The friendliest, most approachable of the group.

**Signature color:** Teal/Green (#148F77)

**Appearance:** The warmest, most inviting character of the five. Round head, even rounder than the others. Enormous sparkling eyes (biggest of any character — Disney-princess-level sparkle). Rosy cheeks. Big warm smile showing teeth. Auburn or reddish hair with a little curl on top. Wears a teal sweater or polo shirt. Small round body. Everything about this character radiates "I'm here to help and I'm happy about it."

**Props:** One hand raised in a big enthusiastic wave — the hand is oversized and cartoonishly expressive. The other hand gives a thumbs up. A speech bubble floats nearby with three animated dots (typing indicator) or a friendly "Hi!" A small heart or sparkle may float near the head.

**Pose:** Open, welcoming body language. Leaning slightly toward the viewer. Both arms out in a "come on in!" gesture. Might be slightly bouncing on the toes with eagerness. The most dynamic and approachable pose of the five.

**Expression:** Maximum friendliness. Huge beaming smile, wide sparkling eyes, raised eyebrows, rosy cheeks. The face of someone who just heard your question and is genuinely delighted to help. Think: the most enthusiastic customer service person you've ever met, but sincerely.

**Prompt snippet:** "Cartoon mascot character, big-headed ultra-friendly character with enormous sparkling eyes, rosy cheeks, huge beaming smile, auburn hair with curl on top, teal sweater, waving with oversized hand, thumbs up with other hand, speech bubble with dots, maximum warmth and friendliness, thick black outlines, vector illustration style, white background"

## Character 5: The Debate Arena

**Role:** Facilitates multi-model discussions where different AI perspectives examine the same clinical question. Two or more models review evidence and challenge each other's reasoning.

**Signature colors:** Blue (#2471A3) vs. Purple (#7D3C98), with gold/yellow (#F1C40F) energy between them.

**Appearance:** This is a two-character composition (not a single mascot). Two distinct big-headed cartoon characters face each other in a spirited debate. Character A (blue) is animated and emphatic — leaning forward, finger pointing. Character B (purple) is skeptical and thoughtful — chin in hand, one eyebrow raised. They are different people: different hair, different builds, different expressions, but the same cartoon style. Between them, energy crackles — lightning bolts, sparks, or collision stars in gold/yellow.

**Props:** Each has a speech bubble. Character A's bubble contains a bold exclamation mark (!). Character B's contains a question mark (?). The bubbles collide in the center with a starburst of energy. A small table or podium may be between them. Optionally, a smaller third figure (green, representing the user/moderator) stands between or behind them.

**Pose:** Character A leans aggressively right, pointing emphatically, mouth open mid-argument. Character B leans left, hand on chin, mouth in a skeptical "hmm" shape, one eyebrow cocked higher than the other. The two are mirror-opposed, creating dynamic tension. The composition should feel like a freeze-frame from an animated argument.

**Expression:** Character A: passionate conviction — wide eyes, emphatic eyebrows, open mouth. Character B: thoughtful skepticism — narrowed eyes, one raised eyebrow, closed-mouth smirk. Together they convey rigorous intellectual debate, not hostility.

**Prompt snippet:** "Cartoon illustration of two big-headed mascot characters in spirited debate, one in blue leaning forward pointing emphatically with speech bubble containing exclamation mark, one in purple leaning back stroking chin skeptically with speech bubble containing question mark, lightning bolts and sparks between colliding speech bubbles, thick black outlines, vector illustration style, white background"

## Usage Notes

**As UI avatars (64x64 to 128x128):** Crop to head and upper body. The oversized heads and distinctive props/colors should make each character instantly recognizable even at small sizes.

**As full illustrations (architecture docs, onboarding):** Use full-body versions with props and background elements.

**As notification icons:** The Narrator's quill, the Analyst's magnifying glass, the Sentinel's radar waves, the Assistant's speech bubble, and the Debate Arena's colliding speech bubbles can each work as standalone icons extracted from the characters.

**Animation potential:** Each character has a natural idle animation implied by their pose — the Narrator writing, the Analyst's lightbulb pulsing, the Sentinel's radar waves scanning, the Assistant waving, the Debate Arena's sparks crackling.
